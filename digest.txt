* COP31: Key outcomes agreed at the UN climate talks in Antalya
  Carbon Brief | 23 Nov 16:15 UTC
  The COP31 climate talks in Antalya, Turkey, have failed to reach agreement on a “pact” that lacked clear references to… The post COP31: Key outcomes agreed at the UN climate talks in Antalya appeared first on Carbon Brief .
  FULL TEXT: The COP31 climate talks in Antalya, Turkey, have failed to reach agreement on a “pact” that lacked clear references to curbing fossil fuels or scaling up climate finance . The “Antalya pact”, proposed by the joint Turkish-Australian presidency, was rejected in the final hours by the EU, the UK and several climate-vulnerable nations. Meanwhile, many developing countries criticised the lack of progress on climate finance and indecision on a proposed $120bn target for funding climate adaptation. With stark divisions preventing agreement on the presidency-led pact, UNFCCC chief Simon Stiell expressed “deep disappointment”, speaking of “scope to improve, modernise and reform” the UN climate process. Despite a chaotic endgame that saw the overrunning COP become the longest in history, it did not end in complete failure, with many items adopted in the closing plenary. These included fairly limited or procedural outcomes on a new “ mechanism ” for global “ just transitions ”, plus support for countries struck by climate disasters. There was some progress beyond the negotiations, though, with growing coalitions of nations championing “ roadmaps ” for “transitioning away” from fossil fuels.
