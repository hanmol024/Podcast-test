Politics.

United States. The Senate passed a permitting reform bill last week that speeds environmental reviews, limits litigation delays and eases power line siting for clean energy and oil and gas alike. Democrats voted two to one against it, yet it reaches a House they take over in January. A new executive order makes contractors withhold anything the White House calls privileged from Congress, which is oversight by permission slip.

UK. Stephen Timms's review of disability payments, published today, calls for a fundamental overhaul of assessments and quicker payment for some claimants, after the government watered down cuts last year. Europe. A Russian drone violated Polish airspace days after cruise missiles fell on the country, and NATO is sending early-warning aircraft. Secretary general Rutte sees Trump at Mar-a-Lago on Wednesday.
